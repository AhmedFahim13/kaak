I see you.

Kaak doesn’t block your phone. He shows up only when scrolling stops being a choice.

A petty Dhaka crow who lives in your phone and your browser. Messages, calls, maps, money apps and learning are left alone. Reels at 2am are not.

The usual screen-time appKaak
Locks everything when a timer runs outOnly interrupts mindless scrolling
Nags with notifications you learn to swipe awayHeckles, steals and eats your feed. You’ll laugh, then stop
Takes your time awayGives it back: walks, water and calls earn minutes
Often sends your data somewhereNo internet permission. Nothing leaves your phone

How it goes

The longer you scroll, the pettier he gets.

  1. After 10 minutes

    He lands.

    Stares. Pecks the glass. Lets you know he’s noticed.

    I see you.

  2. After 15 minutes

    He heckles.

    Walks across your feed. Sits on the like button. Leaves a mess you have to wipe off.

    bro, it’s been 15 minutes

  3. Come back too soon

    He steals it.

    Grabs the app and flies off with it. Every feed stays locked until you tap him five times.

    mine now.

  4. Hit your daily limit

    He eats your feed.

    Three bites, one burp. Then he naps on it for 20 minutes.

    burp. delicious.

  5. Try to sneak back in

    He makes you say it.

    Hold him for 20 seconds. Type the sentence. You get five minutes.

    fine. I’ll remember this.

Bribes accepted.

Earn your minutes back by doing something real. He checks. He’s a crow, not an idiot.

acceptable.

  • Go for a walkYour phone counts the steps.
  • Drink a glass of waterShow him the full glass, then the empty one.
  • Do twenty squatsHe counts every rep. He’s judging your form.
  • Call someone you loveHe waits. Quietly, for once.
  • Put the phone downScreen off, timer running. The easiest one, apparently the hardest.

He can’t phone home. He doesn’t have one.

Kaak has no internet permission. No account, no server, no ads. He counts how long you scroll and how fast, never what you see or who you talk to. Your stats leave your phone only if you send them yourself.

Put it down. Or don’t. He’ll be here.
